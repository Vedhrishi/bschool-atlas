Your complete MBA picture in 60 seconds. I built B-School Atlas to answer "is an MBA worth it?" with numbers, not opinions.

Every MBA applicant asks the same questions: where should I study, which colleges can my score get me into, and how long until the degree pays for itself. The answers are scattered across dozens of sites. So I put them in one place.

What it does:
- A world map of MBA destinations with sourced fees and salaries (ISB, LBS, INSEAD, Stanford GSB, IE, CEIBS and more)
- A 60-second complete picture: enter your salary, pick a destination, see payback, value added, annual return, break-even salary and a verdict — all at once
- A ranking of where your salary pays back fastest
- Indian B-schools by profile: enter your exam score, category, gender, salary and budget to see which colleges are within reach, their fees, average salaries and how many years each takes to pay back for you
- Shortlist, side-by-side comparison and a fee-vs-salary chart
- A 10-year, month-by-month table of approximate fees, salaries and exchange rates, so the numbers stay useful for years
- Available in English, Hindi, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Chinese and Arabic

Every figure shows its source, and nothing you type leaves your device.

Try it: https://vedhrishi.github.io/bschool-atlas/

Cut-offs and fees are approximate and change every year, so always confirm with the college. I'd love feedback from anyone who has been through admissions.
